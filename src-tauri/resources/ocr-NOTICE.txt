The OCR models in this directory are PP-OCRv6 detection/recognition weights,
converted to MNN's .mnn format and redistributed by the ocr-rs project.

  Source:  https://github.com/zibo-chen/ocr-rs (models/)
  Revision: 664a945953e46186b37203fa4902a20d60a32809
  Upstream models: PaddleOCR PP-OCRv6 (https://github.com/PaddlePaddle/PaddleOCR)
  License: Apache License 2.0 — https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0

files:
  PP-OCRv6_small_det.mnn   4,965,224 bytes  text detection
  PP-OCRv6_small_rec.mnn  10,646,760 bytes  text recognition
  ppocr_keys_v6_small.txt     74,947 bytes  recognition charset

The app reads them through the `xphoneuse-ocr` crate, which looks in
<resources>/ocr unless XPHONEUSE_OCR_MODELS points somewhere else (a checkout
of ocr-rs has the same three filenames under models/).
