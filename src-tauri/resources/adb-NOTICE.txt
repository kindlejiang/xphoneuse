adb is bundled from Google's Android SDK Platform-Tools.

  Source:  https://developer.android.com/tools/releases/platform-tools
  Version: 37.0.1 (see adb-source.properties)
  License: Apache License 2.0 — https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0

per-platform files:
  adb            macOS (universal: x86_64 + arm64)
  adb.exe        Windows, with AdbWinApi.dll and AdbWinUsbApi.dll beside it

The app prefers an adb it can find on the machine (an Android SDK install, or
one on PATH) and falls back to these.
